Stage 01 · Brief
What the logo has to do
Brand discovery · constraints

Every design decision below traces back to this brief. If something doesn't serve these specific constraints, it gets cut — no matter how nice it looks in isolation.

Brand position

AI-native growth & systems studio for UK SMEs (£200k–£3m). Sells AI calling agents, custom operational software, conversion websites, AI-optimised paid traffic.

Acquisition

100% word-of-mouth. The logo is a credibility check, not a cold conversion device. Existing clients refer; prospects Google; 90 seconds to look serious.

Outcome promise

The name itself is the offer: "booked" = busy with customers. The logo should carry that meaning visually, not just lexically.

Tone

Confident, not cute. Specific, not generic. Crafted, not generated. Serious with one moment of character.

Constraints — hard bans

No squircle/app-icon shapes. No single-letter monograms with red notification dots. No default Inter/Archivo wordmarks. Nothing that reads as a template.

Must survive

Header at 22px. Browser tab at 16×16. Social avatar at 96×96. Print at letterhead scale. Single colour. Reversed. If it breaks at any size, it loses.

Decision · brief locked, all design serves these constraints
Stage 02 · Type exploration
Which typeface carries the y and the b
4 candidates · 1 chosen

The wordmark is just y, the custom L, and b. The typeface only renders two letters — so they have to be the right two. Tested four candidates, looking for a face with geometric character that allows a custom red L to slot in without looking borrowed.

CANDIDATE 01 · WINNERIBM Plex Sans 700
y b

Wins. Engineered, deliberate, has the stroke-weight precision a custom L can match. The y has a clean diagonal descender; the b's bowl is structurally simple — both leave room for the L's character to lead.

CANDIDATE 02Syne 800
y b

Considered, modern, slightly idiosyncratic. The y's tail is too decorative against a custom L — they fight each other. Strong runner-up, but loses to Plex on neutrality.

CANDIDATE 03Plus Jakarta Sans 800
y b

Clean, modern, very widely used. Loses because it's become the default "I'm a startup" sans — the brief explicitly bans template-feel. No distinction.

CANDIDATE 04Recoleta 700
y b

Warm serif option. Genuinely beautiful but pulls the brand toward editorial/luxury, which doesn't match "AI-native ship-it agency." Wrong category signal.

Decision · IBM Plex Sans 700 — engineering precision, no template baggage
Stage 03 · The L
Six iterations of the custom L
Stem · foot · ratio · optical balance

Earlier attempts failed because the L was either too plain (read as "i") or badly proportioned (foot too thin, stem too short). Working through the proportions deliberately:

01BASELINE

Plain stem. The v1 problem — reads as a dotless i. Rejected.

02THIN FOOT

Foot exists but is weight-mismatched to the stem. Reads as fragile / unfinished.

03OVERSIZED FOOT

Foot now too long — drowns the stem, reads as an arrow not a letter.

04EQUAL WEIGHT

Stem and foot now same stroke weight. Better, but the foot still feels short.

05 · WINNERBALANCED L

Chosen. Stem 18u × 100u tall. Foot 38u × 18u — same weight as stem, 2.1× stem width. Reads as L at every size.

06ROUNDED CORNER

As 05 but as a single path. Same shape, simpler SVG output. Production form.

Decision · iteration 05 (production form = 06) — stem 18u, foot 38×18u
Stage 04 · Construction
The L's geometry, locked
All decisions traced to a number

The mark is built on a strict grid. Every measurement is recorded so any future application (favicon, app icon, stationery, billboard) can be reconstructed identically. No optical guessing.

L construction · specifications
  • Stem width 18u
  • Stem height 100u
  • Foot width 38u
  • Foot height 18u
  • Foot : stem ratio 2.1 : 1
  • Vertical alignment cap height
  • Stroke weight match = IBM Plex 700
  • Corner radius 0 (squared)
  • Negative space (L–b) 14u
  • Negative space (y–L) 14u
Decision · construction grid locked, no further proportional adjustment

Stage 06 · The wordmark, assembled
All decisions in one shape
Plex 700 · custom L · YLB Red

The wordmark with every prior decision applied: IBM Plex Sans 700 for the y and b, the iteration-05 custom L in YLB Red, with locked 14u negative space either side. The L sits at cap height (same as the b's ascender, slightly above the y's x-height) so it reads as a deliberate stylised lowercase l, not a missing letter.
