Receipts · Logo Exploration · 2026

Five directions.

Genuinely distinct visual families, not variants of the same idea. Pick by gut.

A Geometric grid construction

Strength

Mathematical, ownable, distinctive at any size. The grid feels both retro (thermal-print dot matrix) and technical (CSS grid).

Risk

Could read as 8-bit / video-game-y to some, less editorial than other directions.

B Negative-space document mark

Strength

Smart concept executed in pure geometry. Stacked documents create depth. Reads as "a pile of receipts" without being literal.

Risk

Stacked rectangles could read as filing cabinets / corporate / accounting.

C Editorial serif italic

Strength

Maximum editorial confidence. Stripe Press / Anthropic / Pentagram tier. Premium, considered, intellectual.

Risk

Demands the entire site voice match editorial tone. Harder typographic discipline to maintain. Could feel old-school in mono contexts.

D Vertical wordmark with tear-line

Strength

The composition IS the receipt. Vertical mono + perforated tear-line below = signature device built into the logo.

Risk

Vertical doesn't deploy well — most logo contexts are horizontal (nav bars, signatures, mastheads).

E Lettermark system (R. + wordmark)

Strength

Most complete identity SYSTEM. Big R is favicon, social avatar, hero moment. Wordmark beneath handles legal contexts. Hairline = subtotal line. Operator-grade flexibility.

Risk

Two pieces means two ways to use it incorrectly. Needs brand guidelines to deploy consistently.
